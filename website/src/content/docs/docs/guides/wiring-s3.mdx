The prototype of the all-in-one clock: **one ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 (N16R8)** runs
the alarm, Wi-Fi, AirPlay 2, the amplifier and the light strip, in Rust. The
classic ESP32 (Bluetooth audio only) is a companion and is **not needed** for
this page; it is described in the [future board bench](../wiring-bench/).

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 (N16R8) | alarm, Wi-Fi, AirPlay 2, I²S master |
| InnoMaker HiFi AMP Pro Mini (MA12070P) | I²S slave, I²C `0x20`, 9–24 V |
| 2 × 2.2 kΩ | I²C pull-ups to 3V3 |
| WS2812B strip (60 LED), 330 Ω | dawn, sunset, breathing; separate 5 V supply |
| Amplifier supply | regulated 19–20 V, ≥ 4 A, 5.5 mm jack |
| Speakers | 4–8 Ω, **bridged (BTL)** |

:::danger[Rules]
- Wire with everything **unplugged**. Power the amplifier **last**; never hot-plug its supply.
- A speaker "−" is never connected to GND.
- Never connect the HAT's 5 V pins (2 and 4): it outputs 5 V and would fight the devkit's USB.
- One common ground between the devkit and the amplifier.
:::

## Which USB port

The devkit has two USB-C ports. Use the one marked **UART** (WCH serial chip): it
flashes without touching the BOOT and RESET buttons and carries the log console.
The other one (native USB) needs BOOT + RESET to flash.

| Devkit pin | Signal | Amplifier pin |
|---|---|---|
| IO6 | I²S bit clock | 12 |
| IO7 | I²S word clock | 35 |
| IO8 | I²S data to the amplifier | 40 |
| IO17 | I²C SDA (2.2 kΩ to 3V3) | 3 |
| IO18 | I²C SCL (2.2 kΩ to 3V3) | 5 |
| IO21 | enable, high = on | 8 |
| IO41 | mute, low = muted | 10 |
| IO38 | error (input, internal pull-up) | 16 |
| GND | ground | 6 |

IO6, IO7 and IO8 are the same nets as on `robine_board`: the firmware runs
unchanged on the board, except the amplifier chip (TAS5825M there).

IO9 is free on `robine_board` (IO13, used on the classic ESP32, is the shared
SPI MISO there). The signal leaves SPI2 through DMA, so Wi-Fi and AirPlay never
distort it.

**Put a 74AHCT1G125 buffer on the data line**, powered by the strip's 5 V (IO9 →
buffer → 330 Ω → strip). A WS2812B wants at least 0.7 × VDD (3.5 V) on its data
input and the S3 gives 3.3 V: a simulation of the whole board
([`system.cir`](https://github.com/laibulle/robine-wake/tree/main/hardware/perfboard_s3/spice))
shows a margin of 0.04 V without the buffer and 1.6 V with it. The same
simulation moved the I²C pull-ups to 2.2 kΩ (the bus rises in 470 ns instead of
the 1000 ns limit) and replaced the diode that powers the ESP32 from the S3's
USB with a jumper (a diode drops its 3V3 to 2.86 V on a weak USB port).

## Checks before the first power-up

1. **Amplifier alone**, powered: SDA and SCL (pins 3 and 5) read 0 V or 3.3 V,
   **never 5 V**.
2. Unpowered, with a continuity tester, confirm that pins 8, 10 and 16 go to the
   MA12070P's enable, mute and error functions. They come from the Linux
   `merus-amp` overlay and are not confirmed on this board. If one is wrong,
   leave that wire out and say so: the firmware's polarities are the constants
   `ENABLE_ACTIVE` and `MUTE_ACTIVE` in `src/amp.rs`.
3. Keep the volume low on the source for the first sound.

## What the firmware does

- At boot it raises enable, waits 100 ms and reads the amplifier's I²C address
  `0x20`. **If nothing answers it logs one warning and carries on**: the alarm,
  Wi-Fi, API and AirPlay control keep running, only without sound. It tries again
  every 10 s, so the amplifier can be powered later.
- Once it answers, I²S runs at 44.1 kHz, 32-bit stereo, and the amplifier is
  unmuted. The amplifier keeps its power-up settings; the volume is applied to
  the samples.
- While an alarm rings, a fixed 880 Hz beep replaces AirPlay audio (an alarm
  must be audible whatever the AirPlay volume says).
- The light strip runs the same programs as before (`light.set`, `light.program`: dawn, sunset, breathing), sent by the app straight to the S3's API.
- If the I²S output stalls, it mutes, restarts and checks the amplifier again.
- The error line is only logged, never acted on: a false error must not mute
  the alarm.

Expected log line when everything is wired:

```
amplifier: MA12070P answers at I2C 0x20
amplifier: I2S running at 44100 Hz, unmuted
```
